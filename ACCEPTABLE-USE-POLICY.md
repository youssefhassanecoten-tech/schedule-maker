Issued by the copyright holder and repository owner, Youssef Hassan.

This policy applies to Schedule Maker, to every copy of it, and to every document
it produces.

## 1. Purpose

Schedule Maker is a proprietary, internal tool for preparing weekly schedules,
monthly schedules and auditorium requests. It is provided for authorised,
non-commercial work. It is not a public or commercial product.

## 2. Authorised personnel

**Authorised personnel** means the copyright holder and the persons the copyright
holder has authorised in writing to use the Software.

Everyone else is **unauthorised personnel**. This includes, without limitation,
students, applicants, external contractors, guests, former staff and anyone who
was not given that permission in writing. Obtaining a copy, the source code or a
link does not make anyone authorised.

## 3. Permitted use

Authorised personnel may use Schedule Maker **only** for internal, non-commercial
scheduling work, and only on data they are entitled to process.

## 4. Prohibited use

Unauthorised personnel must not, and authorised personnel must not without the
prior written permission of the copyright holder:

- open, copy, download, mirror, host or redistribute the application, its source
  code or any generated document;
- process any other organisation's or person's data with it;
- use it **commercially** — including selling, licensing, sublicensing, renting,
  offering it as a paid product or service, using it to deliver paid work, or
  bundling it into a commercial offering;
- present it as their own work or remove copyright, licence or attribution
  notices;
- share access, exported ZIP files or generated documents with unauthorised
  personnel.

## 5. Personal data

Schedule files can contain personal data such as names, group numbers and room
assignments. Authorised personnel must:

- keep exports on controlled storage and send them only to the addresses that
  need them;
- not publish schedules, screenshots or ZIP files on public sites or messenger
  groups;
- on a shared computer, use a private browser profile and clear site data after
  use (see `SECURITY.md`).

## 6. Security incidents

Report suspected unauthorised access, leaked documents or a vulnerability as
described in `SECURITY.md`. Do not try to investigate by copying data to another
device or service.

## 7. Enforcement

The copyright holder may withdraw authorisation and access at any time. Repeated
or commercial misuse may be pursued under applicable law. Nothing in this policy
limits the rights of the copyright holder.

permission it describes is applied. Where they differ, `LICENSE` prevails.
