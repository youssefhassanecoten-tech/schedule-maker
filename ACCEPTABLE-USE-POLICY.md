# Acceptable Use and Commercial-Use Policy

This policy applies to the Schedule Maker deployment used by the Department of
Russian as a Foreign Language, to every copy of it, and to the departmental
schedule data processed with it.

## 1. Purpose

Schedule Maker is an internal tool for preparing the weekly schedule, the monthly
schedule and the auditorium request (заявка) of the department. It is provided to
make that work faster and less error-prone, not as a public or commercial product.

## 2. Authorised personnel

**Authorised personnel** means the copyright holder and the department staff who
are explicitly permitted by the head of the department to prepare, review or
correct the department's schedules.

Everyone else is **unauthorised personnel**. This includes, without limitation,
students, applicants, external contractors, guests, former staff and anyone who
was not given that permission in writing.

## 3. Permitted use

Authorised personnel may use Schedule Maker **only** for the department's own
non-commercial work: preparing, checking, correcting and distributing the
department's schedule documents.

## 4. Prohibited use

Unauthorised personnel must not, and authorised personnel must not without the
prior written permission of the copyright holder:

- open, copy, download, mirror, host or redistribute the application, its source
  code or any generated document;
- process any other organisation's or person's data with it;
- use it **commercially** — including selling, licensing, sublicensing, renting,
  offering it as a paid product or service, using it to deliver paid work, or
  bundling it into a commercial offering;
- present it as their own work or remove copyright, licence or attribution
  notices;
- share access, exported ZIP files or generated `.docx` documents with
  unauthorised personnel.

## 5. Personal data

The source grid contains staff names and teaching assignments. Authorised
personnel must:

- keep exports on department-controlled storage and send them only to the
  addresses that need them;
- not publish schedules, screenshots or ZIP files on public sites or messenger
  groups;
- on a shared computer, use a private browser profile and clear site data after
  use (see `SECURITY.md`).

## 6. Security incidents

Report suspected unauthorised access, leaked documents or a vulnerability as
described in `SECURITY.md`. Do not try to investigate by copying data to another
device or service.

## 7. Enforcement

Access may be withdrawn at any time. Repeated or commercial misuse may be
reported to the department and the university administration and pursued under
applicable law. Nothing in this policy limits the rights of the copyright holder.

## 8. Relationship to the software licence

The source code is published under the MIT licence (see `LICENSE`). That licence
governs the code. This policy governs access to and use of **the departmental
deployment, the department's data and the Schedule Maker name and branding**,
and grants no additional rights to the code.

- Посторонним лицам запрещено открывать, копировать, распространять программу
  и созданные документы.
- Коммерческое использование (продажа, платные услуги, перепродажа) запрещено
  без письменного разрешения правообладателя.
- Расписания содержат персональные данные: храните их во внутреннем хранилище

- Об инцидентах и уязвимостях сообщайте согласно `SECURITY.md`.
