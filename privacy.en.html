Privacy Policy

Schedule Maker · effective 29 October 2026

By using this site you agree to this Privacy Policy and to the Terms of Use. If you do not agree, do not use the site.

1. About this policy

Schedule Maker is a static web application for preparing schedules. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the site and the app, where it is stored and what rights you have. The owner and copyright holder is Youssef Hassan (the “Owner”).

2. What data is processed

  • the .docx schedule files you upload;
  • application settings (for example rooms, semester dates, holidays);
  • run history, including a saved copy of the uploaded file.

Uploaded files can contain personal data such as staff names, group numbers, rooms and other information present in the schedule.

3. Where and how it is processed

All processing happens locally in your browser. Files are not sent to a server, to the Owner or to any third party. The application has no accounts and uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or telemetry. Uploaded documents are treated as data; they are not executed or evaluated as code.

4. Local storage

Settings are stored in localStorage and run history in IndexedDB of your browser. This data stays on your device until you remove it through the Memory tab, your browser settings or by clearing site data.

5. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process standard technical data (for example IP address and browser details) under its own privacy policy. The Owner does not access those logs and does not link them to you.

6. Your responsibilities

  • use the app only with data you are entitled to process;
  • on a shared computer, use a private browser profile and clear data afterwards;
  • do not publish exported documents or schedule screenshots publicly.

7. Your rights

You can delete local data at any time by clearing site storage or the run history. Questions about data processing can be asked via the project on GitHub.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available on this page; the effective date is shown in the header.
